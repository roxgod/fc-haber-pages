Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

FC Haber Botu (“we”, “us”, or “the service”) automates the preparation and publishing of football news content. This policy describes what information is used when you connect a supported platform.

Information and OAuth authentication

When you authorize a platform, we may receive OAuth credentials and account identifiers needed to perform the actions you approve. OAuth tokens are used only to authenticate requests to the connected service. We do not ask for your platform password.

Token security

Access and refresh tokens are stored in the application’s protected credential storage and are not intended for public pages, logs, source control, or third parties. Access is limited to the publishing workflow. You can revoke authorization through the relevant platform at any time.

Platform API use

  • TikTok: the TikTok Content Posting API may be used to upload approved MP4 content and manage the associated publishing workflow.
  • YouTube: the YouTube API may be used to upload approved videos and set the requested visibility and metadata.
  • Instagram: Instagram publishing integrations may be used to submit approved content and related captions.

Each platform processes data under its own privacy policy and API terms. We do not control how those platforms retain or display content after submission.

Personal data and sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not rent or trade OAuth credentials. Data may be sent to a platform only when required to carry out an authorized publishing action, or to service providers configured by the operator to support that workflow.

Content and retention

The service may retain publishing metadata, source URLs, captions, status records, and error information needed for deduplication, retries, and auditability. The operator may remove local records and revoke platform access according to the deployment setup.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@fchaberbotu.com.
